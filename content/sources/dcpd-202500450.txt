Q. What is your credibility like? Because you said that these tariffs would go in effect. On Monday, you said no pause. And today there is a pause.

Q. So how can people believe your word today, sir?

The President. It's—yes, it's not a question of that. You have to have flexibility. I could say: "Here's a wall, and I'm going to go through that wall. I'm going to go through it no matter what, and keep going"—and you can't go through the wall. Sometimes you have to be able to go under the wall, around the wall, or over the wall. These guys know that better than anybody, right? You got to go around them sometimes; you're not going to go through them. [Laughter]

And I think the word would be "flexible." You have to be flexible. Like, he's asking the question about "some companies." Some companies, through no fault of their own, they happen to be in an industry that is more affected by these things than others. You have to be able to show a little flexibility, and I'm able to do that.
