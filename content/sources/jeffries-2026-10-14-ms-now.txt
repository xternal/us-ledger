House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries on MS NOW's The Weeknight, 14 October 2026: transcript published by the Office of the Democratic Leader as "LEADER JEFFRIES ON MS NOW: “WE'RE GOING TO RELENTLESSLY FOCUS ON OUR AFFORDABILITY AGENDA”".
Copied on 21 October 2026 from https://democraticleader.house.gov/media/press-releases/leader-jeffries-ms-now-were-going-relentlessly-focus-our-affordability-agenda; only the passages this site quotes, each a whole paragraph as published (one no-break space written as a plain space). The second answers a question about the first 100 days of a Democratic House majority.

LEADER JEFFRIES: No, I appreciate that, Michael. We've got to run through the finish line and try to run up the numbers as high as we can, of course, so that we're in a position to decisively govern on behalf of the American people. Like, when we've made clear that we are fighting for an affordable America, that we are committed to driving down the high cost of groceries and gas and housing, that we've committed to fighting to make healthcare affordable for every single American, we mean it when we say it and we want to be able to do it. And the more robust the majority, the better we'll be able to overcome Republican obstruction in ways that, of course, will be designed just to stop progress. And we have an opportunity to be able to win big in the House, in the Senate, in governors races all across the country, certainly in state legislative bodies, where we can flip seats throughout America. And—but we can't take anything for granted because we know the other side is going to throw up every possible obstacle to try to stop this from being a free and fair election, which is why we've put together the largest voter protection effort in modern American history.

LEADER JEFFRIES: Well, what we've made clear is that we're going to relentlessly focus on our affordability agenda. There are some things that we are going to have to do immediately to reverse a lot of the damage that's been done. We're going to fight to reverse these Medicaid cuts. We're going to extend the Affordable Care Act tax credits, which will help make health insurance premiums more affordable for over 20 million Americans directly and tens of millions more whose premiums have increased indirectly, even if they may be on private insurance. That has to be dealt with. We're going to fight to end this reckless and costly war of choice in Iran, which we know has skyrocketed gas prices on the American people. Now, the average gas price being north of $4 for an extended record period of time, that's outrageous. And we also need to make sure we reclaim congressional authority in terms of tariffs so we can end these Trump Tariffs, which have also increased costs on everyday Americans in terms of groceries and goods by thousands of dollars per year. We also are going to focus on accountability because when you have folks in high office who are so focused on enriching themselves, their family members, their friends and their donors, then they're not focused on making life better for the American people. And so, we've made clear to anyone out there who's been stealing from the American people, shortchanging the American people or screwing over the American people that, beginning on day one, we're going to hold the crooks accountable and disrupt business as usual.
