JEN PSAKI: There hasn't been a lot that's come out of that meeting, but Don Leonard, who's a new incoming Member, did tweet, 'Sitting in a small theater with President Obama and the new Members-elect. Someone asked him about Elon Musk. He told us now is the time to amend our constitution. Now is the time. We are the people.' I assume the context of that is Citizens United. Is that the context of that?

LEADER JEFFRIES: Yeah, in fact, I've made clear over the last several months that one of the high priorities that we'll have as House Democrats is to get unregulated big money out of politics. Enough. Now, thankfully, in this most recent cycle, notwithstanding the fact that we've seen MAGA billionaires spend hundreds of millions of dollars trying to steal or trying to buy a Republican majority in the House or in the Senate, that the people of this great country have made clear America is not for sale. But we still have Citizens United out there. And we still have the ability of these MAGA billionaires to flood into our election all of this unregulated money, not designed to help out everyday Americans or to bring about enlightened policy, but to maintain advantage and privilege for the wealthy, the well-off and the well-connected. And so, I think President Obama has been clear that we've got to aggressively tackle Citizens United. That's certainly something that, as House Democrats, we plan to do, and plan to do very early in the new Congress.
