House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries at a press conference, 17 November 2026: remarks published by the Office of the Democratic Leader as "LEADER JEFFRIES: "WE'RE GOING TO FIGHT HARD TO MAKE LIFE MORE AFFORDABLE FOR THE AMERICAN PEOPLE"".
Copied on 22 November 2026 from https://democraticleader.house.gov/media/press-releases/leader-jeffries-were-going-fight-hard-make-life-more-affordable-american; only the paragraph this site quotes, whole as published (each no-break space written as a plain space).

We're going to fight hard to make life more affordable for the American people, and we're going to bring about the type of accountability that is necessary in the face of the unprecedented corruption that MAGA extremists have visited upon the American people. House Democrats are committed to ending this reckless and costly war of choice in Iran. We're committed to stopping the Trump Tariffs that have increased costs on everyday Americans by thousands of dollars per year. And we're committed to getting big money out of politics by ending Citizens United.
