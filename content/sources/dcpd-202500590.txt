Remarks on Signing an Executive Order on Delivering Most-Favored-Nation Prescription Drug Pricing to American Patients and an Exchange With Reporters, 12 May 2025 (Daily Compilation of Presidential Documents, DCPD-202500590).
Copied on 20 October 2026 from https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202500590/html/DCPD-202500590.htm; only the passages this site quotes, each a whole paragraph as published.

Some prescription drug and pharmaceutical prices will be reduced almost immediately by 50 to 80 to 90 percent. Big Pharma will either abide by this principle voluntarily or we'll use the power of the Federal Government to ensure that we are paying the same price as other countries.
