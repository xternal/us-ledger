The President, "The American Declaration of Freedom in Space", a proclamation of 3 November 2026, published by the White House.
Copied on 8 November 2026 from https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/11/the-american-declaration-of-freedom-in-space/; only the paragraph this site quotes, whole as published (each no-break space written as a plain space, and the two spaces after each sentence as one).

This proud legacy of innovation is our sacred inheritance, and our Nation bears the solemn responsibility to advance it by continuing to open new horizons of exploration and discovery. Through the landmark Executive Orders and directives I have signed, we are reinvigorating human spaceflight, unleashing a competitive and thriving commercial space industry, and ensuring American space superiority for all eternity. We will return to the Moon by 2028, lay the foundation for a permanent American lunar presence, and hasten the day when an American becomes the first human to set foot on the red dunes of Mars. Together, we will build the facilities, develop the resources, and establish a permanent presence in space to extend the reach of American exploration, preserve our frontier spirit, and strengthen our national security for centuries to come. The nation that leads in space will shape the future of humanity — and that nation will be the United States of America.
