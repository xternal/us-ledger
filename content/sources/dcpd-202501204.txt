Address to the Nation on the Economy, 17 December 2025 (Daily Compilation of Presidential Documents, DCPD-202501204).
Copied on 20 October 2026 from https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202501204/html/DCPD-202501204.htm; only the passages this site quotes, each a whole paragraph as published.

Because of tariffs, along with the just-passed "One Big Beautiful Bill," tonight I am also proud to announce that more than one thousand four hundred and fifty thousand—think of this: one million four hundred and fifty thousand military servicemembers will receive a special, we call, "warrior dividend" before Christmas—so, warrior dividend. In honor of our Nation's founding in 1776, we are sending every soldier $1,776. Think of that. And the checks are already on the way.
